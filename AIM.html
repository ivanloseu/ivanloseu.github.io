AI for Math

This is a participating seminar organized by Ivan Losev and Ro Wang. Its primary audience is Yale Math graduate students and its goal is to explain how one could and should use AI for Math research and education. Participate are strongly encouraged to make presentations on various aspects of using AI for Math!

Some online resources: Keep in mind that views expressed in these pieces are not necessarily shared by the organizers.

  • Lisa Carbone's note on advancing Math research using AI.
