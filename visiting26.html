Yale graduate visiting day: Mar 23, 2026.

  • On this page you'll find various information regarding Yale's graduate visiting day. The default arrival and departure dates are March 22 (Sunday) and March 24 (Tuesday), respectively. For people with longer commutes we'll consider the departure on March 25, please inquire with Prof. Ivan Loseu if you'd like to use this option. New Haven is a great city and the Yale campus is gorgeous, so you may want to arrive earlier on Sunday and/or depart later on Tuesday. Also, if you depart later on Tuesday there will be more opportunities to interact with faculty and current graduate students. Besides, on March 24, 4-5pm, we plan to have a distinguished lecture by Prof. Zhiwei Yun (MIT).
